The Art of Typography in Web Design

Typography is one of the most essential elements in web design. It influences how users perceive and interact with your content. Using the right combination of font family, size, and spacing can transform a plain paragraph into something engaging and visually pleasing.

Designers often mix serif and sans-serif fonts to create contrast and readability. A well-balanced use of letter-spacing and line-height improves the visual rhythm of your text, making it easier for readers to follow along without feeling overwhelmed.

In modern design, subtle details like first-line emphasis and decorative initials (::first-letter) can help draw attention and give personality to your content.
